Preserve the visual language of the supplied Hanabi references: traditional Japanese woodblock-inspired flat pixel art, ivory paper border, dark indigo night, snow-capped Mount Fuji centered in the distance, calm lake and low foothills. This is a reusable background master, so include NO numeral, NO letters, NO suit icon, NO fireworks, NO text, NO UI, NO perspective tilt, NO hands, and NO mockup shadow. Crisp deliberately pixelated edges and a restrained limited palette; original design rather than copying any exact reference. Put the card on a uniform pure magenta (#FF00FF) chroma-key background outside the rounded card silhouette. The border and all interior artwork must be fully opaque.

## 派生方式

`fimel_cards/tools/generate_hanabi_assets.ps1`以两张透明母版为基础，确定性派生：

- 五种颜色、五个数字的25张完整牌面；
- 五张只显示颜色的提示态；
- 五张只显示数字的提示态；
- 一张统一牌背；
- 256×256物品纹理；字体直接引用同一批纹理，并用负间距处理透明边，不另存重复图片。

数字、颜色带、烟花数量和像素缩放均由脚本绘制，未再次调用图像生成工具。
